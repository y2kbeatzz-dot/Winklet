Everything in one little star

Tiny companion.
Big personality.

From dancing along to Apple Music to watching over your Mac's battery, Winklet makes ordinary desktop moments feel a little more alive.

👁️

Eye Studio

16 styles, different left and right eyes, adjustable colors, pupils, blinking and cursor-following gaze.

🎶

Music moods

React to Apple Music and Spotify playback, switch dance styles, and see full song titles, artists and albums.

🔋

Battery buddy

Charging and unplugged reactions, customizable low-battery warnings, and optional system notifications.

🎨

Make it yours

Hue shift, eye colors, glow, scale, opacity, name, pronouns, animation and expressive reactions.

🪟

Float your way

Optional Always on Top, click-through mode, Spaces support, cursor follow and drag-to-position.

🔐

Your Mac, your data

Local preferences, no analytics or account. Optional microphone and input permissions remain your choice.
