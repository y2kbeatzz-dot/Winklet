How to install

  1. Download the universal DMG from GitHub Releases.
  2. Open the disk image, then drag Winklet.app into Applications.
  3. Launch Winklet. Find its small star in the Mac menu bar to open customization.

The beta is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. macOS may show a security warning. After attempting to open it, check System Settings → Privacy & Security for the option to allow it.

Common questions

I can’t click apps underneath Winklet.

Switch on Click-through in the Desktop tab. This is separate from the Always on Top option; both are optional.

The full song title is hidden.

Turn on the full Now Playing card in Music reactions. Apple Music or Spotify may ask for macOS Automation permission.

Winklet isn't dancing exactly on beat.

Music dancing follows play/pause activity and animation patterns; the beta does not analyze actual audio beats.

No charging or low-battery reaction?

Check the Power & Battery settings and warning threshold. Notifications require separate macOS permission.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. Builds are created automatically on GitHub Actions.

Beta bug reports

Please include your macOS version, Mac processor type, what you expected, what happened, and any visible error text. Don't include private information such as passwords or Apple account details.
