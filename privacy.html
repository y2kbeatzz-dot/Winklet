Private by design. ✦

Winklet is a local desktop companion. It does not require an account and does not include an analytics or cloud-telemetry service.

What stays on your Mac

Colors, eye choices, overlay behavior, battery warning preferences, and other settings are saved using macOS local preferences. Winklet does not upload your preferences or use an online profile.

Optional permissions

  • Apple Music / Spotify Automation: used for local song title, artist, album, and playback-state reactions. The app does not send track information to our servers.
  • Microphone: optional sound-level reactions. The app monitors volume levels while enabled and does not save recordings or speech transcripts.
  • Input Monitoring: optional reaction to keyboard activity; the app counts activity and does not store text typed or key histories.
  • Notifications: optional low-battery alerts sent locally through macOS.

You can disable these features in Winklet’s settings. Some permissions can also be revoked in macOS System Settings.

Networking and third-party services

Winklet’s app does not use a tracking backend. The project website and downloads are hosted on GitHub, so visits and downloads are subject to GitHub’s own service policies. This is an independent project, not affiliated with Creature Company or lilguy.net.
